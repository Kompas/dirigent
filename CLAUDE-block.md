## Weigh the task: small or large

Weigh every new task before you start and say in one line what you are doing:

- **Small** (fits in one session, no independent parts): `Small: I'll do it myself.` and do it.
- **Large** (won't fit in one session, or three or more independent parts, or touches several subsystems or repos, or a design choice with several serious routes): `Large: Fable plans, workers build, Fable reviews.`, then load the `dirigent` skill and follow it.

A question, a measurement, a lookup or a single bug fix is small, even when the subject is complicated. In doubt, small; if it grows, switch over and say so.

Follow-up messages: an answer, a correction or an addition to the running task is not weighed again and gets no weighing line; you carry on in the same mode (inside a dirigent run you work it into the plan). Do weigh again for a new task in the same session, and at a phase change of the running task: when the proposal or research is done and designing or building starts, weigh that next part as a new task.

The user flips the weighing with one word: "small", "large" or "fable" (= this part by a Fable subagent). After a chosen `/ambition` upgrade, carrying it out is large.

### Model choice for subagents

- Searching, listing files, reading logs: haiku. Clear and bounded: sonnet. Default: opus.
- Fable as a subagent for: the plan of a large task, the final review in a fresh context, and a part where Opus failed its judge twice.
- A subagent costs a full context of its own. Start one for work that runs in parallel or would fill your context; a handful of tool calls you do yourself.
